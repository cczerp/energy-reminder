Timbre Tamer privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Timbre Tamer is a reminder and journal app for mindfulness practice. We, the developer, receive none of your data. There are no accounts, and your notes, logs and settings never leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your goals, notes, evening recalls, daily history and settings are saved only inside the app on your phone. If you delete the app or clear its data, they are gone. We cannot see or recover them.

Ads (Google Play version, free tier)

The free version of Timbre Tamer on Google Play shows ads, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app-usage information, and may use cookies or similar technology. We do not give Google your notes, logs, goals or location, and the ad service does not receive them from us. In Europe and the UK the app first asks for your consent. You can change your choice, or reset or turn off personalized ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. See Google's advertising policy and privacy policy.

The one-time purchase that unlocks the advanced teachings also removes the ads. The copy of Timbre Tamer you download directly from our website contains no ads and sends nothing anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Location

If you turn on the "when you leave home" reminders, the app saves the spot where you tapped Set my home here on your phone. It asks your phone's built-in geofencing service to tell it when you leave that area, which is why Android asks for location "all the time". Your location is never sent anywhere, never shared, and not kept as a history. You can switch this off at any time in Settings.

Notifications and alarms

The app schedules reminders on your phone. They are created and shown on the phone itself.

Backups

The backup button in Settings makes a file only when you press it. Where it goes (for example your own email or cloud drive) is up to you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Not medical care

Timbre Tamer supports a practice. It is not medical care and does not replace a counselor or doctor.
