MindCue privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

MindCue is a reminder and journal app for mindfulness practice. It collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and it never sends anything to a server. The app does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, notes, evening recalls and daily history are saved only inside the app on your phone. If you delete the app or clear its data, they are gone. We cannot see or recover them.

Location

If you turn on the "when you leave home" reminders, the app saves the spot where you tapped Set my home here on your phone. It asks your phone's built-in geofencing service to tell it when you leave that area, which is why Android asks for location "all the time". Your location is never sent anywhere, never shared, and not kept as a history. You can switch this off at any time in Settings.

Notifications and alarms

The app schedules reminders on your phone. They are created and shown on the phone itself.

Backups

The backup button in Settings makes a file only when you press it. Where it goes (for example your own email or cloud drive) is up to you.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects nothing from anyone.

Not medical care

MindCue supports a practice. It is not medical care and does not replace a counselor or doctor.
