## Emotion and thought (your example of the style)

0. A thought or feeling cannot be destroyed, only changed. The harder you push it away, the longer it stays. Find the opposite feeling and lean toward it: for fear, look for trust; for anger, look for relief; for sadness, look for something to appreciate.
